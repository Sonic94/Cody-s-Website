OUR STORY

Rooted in Craft. Built on Trust.

Cody's Sawmill was founded on a simple belief: quality timber starts with quality care. What began as a small family operation has grown into a trusted name for lumber and custom milling, without ever losing sight of the values we started with.

Every log that comes through our yard is treated with the same respect, whether it's headed for a backyard deck or a full home build. We take pride in precision, and we take the time to get every cut right.

Our team brings decades of combined experience to the mill floor. We've weathered storms, sharpened our craft, and built relationships with contractors, woodworkers, and homeowners who keep coming back because they know what they're getting: honest work and honest lumber.

Craftsmanship

Every board is cut with care and inspected for quality before it leaves our mill.

Sustainability

We source responsibly and make the most of every log that comes through our yard.

Community

We're proud to serve the builders and makers in our local area, one project at a time.
